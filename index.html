The host sets seats to Open for people to join; empty Open seats are filled by the AI at start. Everyone runs the same simulation and only orders travel over the network, so a desync is detected by hash and repaired from the host.

How to play & hotkeys
  1. Right-click villagers onto trees, berries, sheep, deer, gold and stone. Drop-offs go to the Town Hall or a camp.
  2. Build Houses (+5 pop) and a Barracks. Farms give slow, endless food once berries run out. Boars fight back: hunt with 3+ villagers.
  3. Age up at the Town Hall to unlock archers, horsemen, towers, the market, knights, siege and monks.
  4. Archers beat infantry, horsemen beat archers, spearmen beat horsemen. Rams and catapults wreck buildings. Monks heal and convert.
  5. Raided? Ring the Town Bell to garrison villagers. Win by conquest, by a Wonder standing 5 minutes, or by holding every relic for 5 minutes.
